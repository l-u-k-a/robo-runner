Robo Runner – a retro pixel-art puzzle platformer

Guide a laser-wielding robot through 60 levels of temples, ruins, fortresses, ice caves, sunken cities and neon grids. Dig pits to trap the guards, collect every gold coin and reach the portal. Use tools like shock traps, decoys, smoke bombs, glue and bombs. Play free in your browser with the keyboard: arrows to move and climb, Z and X to dig, Space to use a tool, P to pause.
